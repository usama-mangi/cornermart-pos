CornerMart POS Database Guide

1. Tracking stock without losing history

Instead of only storing a single number for how many items are on the shelf, the database keeps two linked records for every product at every store location.

stock_level holds the live balance right now (qty_on_hand, qty_reserved, and weighted average cost avg_cost) so the cash register can check availability instantly. Every time stock arrives, sells, transfers, or gets adjusted, a permanent signed entry is written to stock_movement. The database blocks qty_reserved from ever exceeding qty_on_hand, so you cannot sell stock you do not have.

2. How orders, holds, and pickup move step by step

Both in-store register sales (POS) and web orders (ONLINE) live in sales_order. Click any stage below to see what happens to the reserved stock and the order history at that moment.

3. Locking prices and taxes at the moment of sale

Catalog prices change, and tax rates have start and end dates in tax_rate. If an order line simply pointed back to the product table, raising the price of milk next week would rewrite last month's receipts.

To prevent that, sales_order_line copies the exact unit_price and tax_rate_pct onto the line item when the order is created. Database rules also require a written discount_reason whenever a cashier applies a discount greater than zero, and verify that line_net + line_tax = line_total.

4. Taking payments and issuing refunds without double-charging

Network drops at the register and duplicate webhook retries from payment gateways are normal in retail. Each payment attempt carries two unique guards in payment: idem_key (stops a retried checkout request from charging twice) and gateway_event_id (stops a repeated gateway callback from being processed twice).

When money is returned, the original payment row is never overwritten. Instead, each partial or full refund is recorded as its own permanent row in refund tied back to the original payment.

5. Locking sensitive records against edits or deletion

Five tables in this database are strictly append-only: stock_movement, order_status_history, refund, app_log, and audit_log. Database triggers reject any UPDATE or DELETE on those tables with error -20901.

Similarly, once an inventory cycle_count reaches CLOSED, a trigger locks the count against further changes (-20902). On all 20 editable tables, triggers automatically stamp who created or changed each row, record UTC timestamps, and increment row_version so two people cannot silently overwrite each other's edits.
